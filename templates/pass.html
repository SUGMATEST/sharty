Don't have a pass? Apply here, they're completely free and serve as a way of whitelisting trusted users.

Using a pass grants the following benefits:

  • No file approval required for new media.
  • No McChallenge captcha screen.
  • Disabled VPN checks, meaning you can post from VPNs.
  • Optional flag on country flag boards which displays how long you've been a pass user for.
  • Access to /gem/, a board only pass users can post on.

To display your country flag instead of your pass on flag boards, set your email to flag.
